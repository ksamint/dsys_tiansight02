# Portraits

Drop the five co-founder portraits here, named in the order they were supplied:

    founder-01.png  founder-02.png  founder-03.png  founder-04.png  founder-05.png

Square crops on a white or transparent background, at least 1200 × 1200 px. The
portrait tile multiplies the image onto the ink field, so a white background
disappears without editing. Until a file exists the tile shows a labelled placeholder.

## Co-founder profiles

`founder-bian-jiang.png` and `founder-guo-feng.png` are the portraits the profile pages
(`people/profile-*.html`) use, extracted from the A4 profile PDFs the founders supplied. They are
388 × 488 and 435 × 542 px, enough for screen and a first print; replace them with 1200 px+ originals
under the same names when available. The profile panel crops to 4 : 5.2 with `object-fit: cover`.
